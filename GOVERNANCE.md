# Governance

Nise & Go is maintained as an owner-led personal project.

## Decision authority

The maintainer decides:

- Product direction and scope.
- Which stacks and profiles are supported.
- Release timing.
- Compatibility policy within the published versioning rules.
- Whether a contribution is reviewed, accepted, revised, or closed.

Issues and discussions may inform decisions but do not constitute votes. Popularity alone does not justify expanding the supported matrix.

## Design changes

Consequential decisions should be documented in `docs/adr/`. A decision may change when evidence from real applications, security work, or benchmarks invalidates it. Reversals must explain the new evidence and migration effect.

## Maintainer availability

No support, review, release, or security-response SLA is promised. Users who require contractual guarantees should maintain their own fork or use a commercially supported alternative.
